# Procurement Policy v3 — Pantry Restocking

## Approved vendors
Pantry restocking must use approved vendors only. The approved merchant for
pantry supplies is **Demo Pantry Co** (https://demo-pantry.example.com).

## Budget
The per-order spending cap for pantry restocking is **$200 USD**. Orders must be
at or under this cap. Orders above the cap require manager sign-off.

## Preferred brands
Preferred brands for the pantry are **Blue Bottle** (coffee), **Clif** (snack
bars), and **LaCroix** (sparkling water). Prefer these brands when available.

## Prohibited items
Do **not** purchase energy drinks. Avoid non-approved brands where a preferred
brand exists.

## Category
Buy pantry staples only: coffee, snack bars, and sparkling water.
